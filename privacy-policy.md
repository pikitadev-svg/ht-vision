# HT Vision — Privacy Policy

HT Vision is a browser extension that runs entirely on your device.

**No data is collected, transmitted, or stored externally.**

- All player data and analytics are computed locally from the Hattrick pages you visit.
- Data is stored only in your browser's local IndexedDB (on your machine, never uploaded).
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry of any kind.
- No account required. No servers. No third parties.

The only external network request made by this extension is when you click the
☕ Support button, which opens ko-fi.com in a new tab — initiated by you, not automatically.
